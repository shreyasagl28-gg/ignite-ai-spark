# Ignite AI Spark

PROJECT: IAIB (Ignite AI Buildathon), Season 01. A free national AI buildathon for Indian school students in Classes 9 to 12, by upGrad School of Technology, supported by the Government of Karnataka.



AUDIENCE: students aged 13 to 17 on mid-range Android phones, plus their parents and school principals. Mobile performance matters more than desktop effects.



DESIGN DIRECTION: premium, minimal, confident, like a global product launch, not a school event. One memorable moment (the hero spark field), everything else quiet.



COLOURS (use only these):

- upGrad Red #E50913 (primary actions, live counter, one key number per section, sparks). Max about 5% of any screen.

- Deep Red #B0070F (hover/pressed)

- Ink #0B0B0C (dark sections), Paper #FAFAF7 (light sections), Paper 2 #F1F1EC

- Greys #6B6B6B, #9A9A9F, lines #E3E3DE (light) and #2A2A2D (dark)

- Spark field only: #E50913 to #FF4D2E to #FF8A3D

Sections alternate dark and light. Partner logos are monochrome.



TYPE: Poppins 600 for headlines (very large, letter-spacing about -0.035em, line-height ~1.02). Inter for body (16 to 18px). JetBrains Mono only for numbers in the live counter, dates and session counts.



MOTION: GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis smooth scroll. Slow, smooth, purposeful. Respect prefers-reduced-motion everywhere.



NEVER:

- No purple, blue or rainbow gradients. No gradient text.

- No drop shadows on cards, no glassmorphism, no emoji icons, no generic icon sets in coloured circles.

- No decorative code snippets or brackets as decoration.

- No fade-and-slide animation on every element. Only the hero, the journey line and the Spark card move.

- No stock photos of people at laptops. Use real photos or leave clean placeholders.

- Do not change the copy. Content comes from the HTML.



CONTENT RULES: Students are minors. Leaderboards and Spark cards show first name and school only. Registration always includes parental consent.
